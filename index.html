Neonatal pig islet microtissues

Homogeneous, scalable pig islet microtissues for diabetes and metabolic research

Human islets are scarce and vary from donor to donor; rodent islets are far from human physiology. We turn neonatal pig islets into islet microtissues of a defined size, from defined donors, delivered the way your assay needs them.

01 · Undefined to defined

Size set by cell number, every time

Islet cells are dissociated and reaggregated in microwells into microtissues of 300 to 1,500 cells. Diameter follows cell number (R² = 0.99).
